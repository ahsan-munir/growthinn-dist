How it goes.

  1. Step 1

    I read what your pages actually emit — not what the theme documentation says they emit, and not what an app’s marketing page claims.

  2. Step 2

    I fix it in the theme. Same person — a schema fix handed to someone else to implement is how duplicate emission happens in the first place.

  3. Step 3

    I validate, then hand you the output. You can re-run it yourself any time; that’s the point of choosing a check a third party owns.
