Privacy

Last updated 8 October 2026

Who I am

Growthinn is Ahsan Munir, working on Shopify stores. If you want anything on this page explained or anything held about you removed, write to me and I’ll do it.

What this site collects

Analytics. I use analytics to see which pages are read and which aren’t. No advertising trackers, no remarketing pixels, no cross-site profiling. You can decline analytics with the notice at the bottom of the page, and declining does not change anything about how the site works.

The calculator. Whatever you type into it stays in your browser.Nothing you enter is sent anywhere — not to me, not to a third party. The arithmetic runs on your own device.

Forms. The store-read intake and the contact form send what you type to me by email, through a form-delivery service. I use the store URL, the sessions figure and anything you tell me to do the thing you asked for. I don’t add you to a mailing list and I don’t send sequences.

Access to your analytics and store data

Diagnosing a store means reading its numbers, sopaid work normally includes read access to your Google Analytics and your Shopify admin reports. Some specifics:

  • I ask for read access, not write access, and only to what the work needs.
  • I use it to establish the baseline the work is measured against, and to find where the funnel is losing orders.
  • I don’t share your figures with anyone else, and I don’t publish them — not as a case study, not anonymised — without your written permission.
  • Access ends when the work does. Ask me to remove myself at any point and I will, including mid-engagement.
  • Where a specialist on my side needs access to do part of the work, I tell you who and why first.

How long I keep things

Enquiries and project correspondence I keep while we might still be working together and for as long as I need them for tax records. Analytics data is aggregated and holds nothing that identifies you.

Your choices

You can decline analytics, ask what I hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. If you’re in the UK or EU, those are rights you have under the GDPR; I’d do the same thing if you weren’t.

If you came here to check whether this is a real operation before sending anything: that’s a reasonable thing to do. Thefree store read asks for a URL, a rough sessions figure and an email address, and nothing else.
