Why this matters

Your traffic isn’t the problem.
The page receiving it is.

A premium theme, a full catalogue, and a healthy ad budget still leave most stores converting under 1%. That’s not bad luck — it’s what happens when the storefront was never built to turn visitors into buyers. Here’s what that quietly costs you.

0.8%

A typical store converts under 1%

Send 1,000 paid visitors to an unoptimised store and you’ll see 8–15 orders. At $3–8 a click, that maths goes underwater fast — and no amount of extra traffic fixes a leaking page.

90%

Most product pages don’t sell

A name, a price, and an Add-to-Cart button isn’t persuasion. Without proof, story, and objection-handling at the decision point, your best traffic bounces — and you blame the ads.

3–5×

The gap a fixed store closes

Stores that optimise for conversion first run 3–5× the conversion rate on the same traffic. That’s the difference between scaling profitably and quietly shutting down within 90 days.

The winners optimise conversion before they scale ads — then scale into a store that pays them back. That’s the whole job of every service above.

Questions

How the work works.

The questions brands ask before they start — about the approach, not the basics.

How do I know which service I actually need?

Often you don't yet — and that's fine. Start with the free audit or a CRO audit; both are built to tell you, straight, where your biggest revenue leak is and which service addresses it. I'd rather point you at the one fix that moves the needle than sell you the whole list.

Can I combine services, or do I have to pick one?

They're designed to stand alone and compound together. A faster theme lifts your technical SEO; cleaner SEO feeds more qualified traffic into the CRO programme; higher conversion lowers your effective ad cost. We usually start where the return is highest and layer the rest as it makes sense.

What makes your approach different from other CRO consultants?

Most consultants audit and disappear, leaving you a PDF to implement with a team you don't have. I do both — diagnose and build. Because the same person writes the recommendation and ships it, the advice is accountable: if it's suggested, it gets built, so it has to actually work.

Do you just give recommendations, or do you implement them?

Implement. The whole model is built against the audit-and-vanish trap. You get working pages, rewritten copy, a live trust stack, and configured tracking — not a list of things to go and do yourself. The audit and the build are one path, not two invoices.

Am I working with you directly, or a team?

Directly. There's no account manager, no queue, and no handoff between a designer who hasn't seen your data and a developer who hasn't seen your buyer. One specialist owns strategy, build, and measurement — which is exactly where agency handoffs tend to lose the result.

How quickly will I see results?

Obvious, high-impact fixes often ship within the first days rather than waiting on a full report, so you feel movement early. Deeper CRO and SEO gains compound over weeks and months. Either way, measurement is set up from the start so you see the change in real numbers, not assumptions.

Who is this actually for?

DTC health, fitness, and beauty brands on Shopify with real traffic that isn't converting like it should. If you're pre-traffic or pre-product, honest answer: you're better served elsewhere first, and I'll tell you so rather than take the project.

What does an engagement typically cost?

It depends on scope — a focused audit is a small fixed engagement; a full build or an ongoing CRO programme is larger. Rather than quote blind, I'll scope it after the audit shows what's actually worth doing, so you're paying for the fixes that pay you back, not a padded retainer.

How do you measure whether the work paid off?

In business metrics, not vanity ones — revenue per visitor, profit per order, qualified organic traffic, Core Web Vitals — tracked before and after. Conversion rate alone can lie; I optimise for the numbers that decide whether your store actually makes more money.

Do I keep ownership of everything you build?

Completely. The pages, the theme, the documented playbook of what was tested and why — all yours, living in your store, editable by your team. The goal is to leave you with a capability that outlasts the engagement, not a dependency on me.

What if I already have an agency or developer?

I often work alongside them, taking the Shopify-specific technical and conversion work generalists aren't set up for and handing back clean foundations for their content, ads, or design to build on. No territory wars — just the part of the problem I'm the specialist for.

What's the first step?

Send me your store for a free audit. I'll come back with where the biggest opportunity is and whether I'm the right person to fix it — with no obligation either way. From there we scope only what's worth doing.
